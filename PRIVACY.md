# Privacy Policy for ClipGhost

**Last updated:** September 2026

ClipGhost is built with privacy as its fundamental architecture.

### 1. Zero-Data Collection
ClipGhost does NOT collect, store, transmit, log, or share any personal data, usage analytics, device identifiers, or clipboard contents.

### 2. Air-Gapped & Offline Execution
All encryption, decryption, and cryptographic hash operations are computed entirely in volatile device memory locally on your hardware. The application requests zero network permissions and makes no outbound network connections.

### 3. Third-Party Access
There are no third-party tracking libraries, advertisements, or cloud analytics integrated into this application.

### 4. Contact
For questions regarding this policy, visit the project repository: https://github.com/kovacsmaxi/clipghost
